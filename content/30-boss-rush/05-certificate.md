You made it to the end. Open your certificate:

**[Open my certificate →](#/certificate)**

Type your name, and BossGit draws your certificate with the number of lessons, missions and problems you finished. Take a screenshot or print it to PDF.

## Make it count

A certificate from a free website will not get you a job by itself. **What you built while earning it will.** Put these on your CV and profile:

- **minigit** and **MiniHub**, with tests, CI and a release (the capstone).
- Your **contribution graph**: real, steady work.
- One or two **open source PRs** that were merged.
- A blog post: "What I learned building Git from scratch".

## Keep your progress safe

Your progress lives in this browser. Go to **[Me](#/me)** and use **Export progress** to download a backup file. Import it on any other device.

```quiz
q: What will impress an interviewer most?
- A certificate image
- [x] The capstone repo with tests, CI and releases, and being able to explain it
why: Proof of skill beats proof of attendance.
```
